What is allowed into a cell.

Every blue input is tied to an EDGAR filing or an issuer exhibit: form, period, statement line, raw amount, unit, and scale. News pages are not Excel inputs. A labeled line that was not pulled stays blank. A blank is not stored as zero.

Definitions

Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capex. EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization. Net debt is current debt plus long-term debt minus cash. Units are millions of USD. Waste Connections prints in thousands and is divided by 1,000. Clean Harbors exhibit lines are stored in thousands and scaled the same way, so 6,030,837 thousand is 6,030.837 million.
